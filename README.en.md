# Engineering Studio 🎛️ (English)

A **local-first** web studio for Teenage Engineering machines (OP-1, OP-1 field, EP-133 K.O. II) and Eurorack modular synthesis. Verified backups, sample editing, Web MIDI sync, and an offline DSP synthesis rack that produces rich audio without requiring physical hardware connected.

## 🎹 15 Synthesis Engines with Interactive Pixel Art Faceplates

1. **Mutable Instruments Plaits (12HP)**: 8 synthesis models (Virtual Analog, Waveshaping, 2-OP FM, Formant, Harmonic, Wavetable, Chord, Particle).
2. **Mutable Instruments Braids (16HP)**: Digital macro-oscillator with OLED algorithm display, Color, Timbre, and Bitdepth controls.
3. **Mutable Instruments Rings (14HP)**: Physical modeling resonator (String, Modal, Membrane modes) with Damping and Position controls.
4. **Mutable Instruments Clouds (18HP)**: Granular texture synthesizer with grain density, pitch shifting, and reverb.
5. **Mutable Instruments Elements (34HP)**: Modal exciter (Bow/Blow/Strike) with Geometry and Brightness controls.
6. **Roland TB-303 Acid Bass**: Diode-ladder filter simulator with Saw/Square waveforms and Accent/Drive.
7. **Dexed FM (Yamaha DX7)**: 6-operator FM synthesis with 32 algorithms and Operator ratio tuning.
8. **Surge XT Hybrid**: Wavetable oscillator with morphing, dual filters, and drive stage.
9. **pl_synth DMG-01**: Retro Nintendo GameBoy / NES chiptune engine with Bitcrush, sample rate divider, and glitch generator.
10. **amsynth**: Vintage Moog-style subtractive synth with dual VCOs and LFO depth.
11. **ZynAddSubFX**: Harmonic additive synthesizer with sub-boost and band-limited harmonic generators.
12. **Helm**: Cross-modulation synthesizer with dual visual LFO modulators and sub-octave support.
13. **FluidSynth SF2**: SoundFont playback engine with Acoustic Grand Piano, Rhodes, Organ, and Strings presets.
14. **AMY Engine**: Fixed-point additive synthesizer with configurable partials and slope decay.
15. **Faust DSP**: Mathematical wavefolding DSP unit with feedback loops and filter resonance.
